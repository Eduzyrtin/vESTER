Your money, your people, your phone. Nothing else.

vESTER is a colourful notebook for your finances that lives entirely on your phone. No bank login, no account, no ads and no internet permission: your numbers never leave your device unless you export them.

★ ACCOUNTS AND TRANSACTIONS
• As many accounts as you like, each with its own colour and emoji
• Payments, income and transfers between accounts
• Categories, notes and receipt photos
• Templates for what you repeat, autocomplete and search by text, amount, date or account
• Undo after deleting instead of "are you sure?" dialogs

★ DEBTS AND SHARED EXPENSES
• Debts to pay and to collect, with due dates and partial payments
• Groups for a trip, a flat or a dinner: split equally or by parts, see each person's balance and settle up with the fewest payments
• PDF report of any debt, ready to send

★ BUDGETS, GOALS AND REPORTS
• Monthly budgets per category with alerts at 80% and 100%
• Savings goals with how much to set aside each month
• Reports by month and year, calendar view and account combination
• Your month can start on payday
• Monthly PDF report

★ SCHEDULED AND RECURRING
• Give a movement a future date and it is logged by itself
• Repeat it every day, week, month or year

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Fingerprint or face lock, hidden in the recents screen
• Discreet mode: balances show as ••••
• Export and import CSV
• Automatic weekly backup to a folder you choose
• Multiple currencies with manual exchange rates

★ FAST TO LOG
• Home-screen widget, Quick Settings tile and launcher shortcuts
• Cash counter for coins and notes
• Optional suggestions from notifications: if you grant notification access, vESTER reads payment notifications only from a short list of wallet, bank and mail apps to offer suggestions you confirm. Nothing else is read or stored, and nothing leaves your device.

Material 3 design, light and dark themes, tablet layouts. Available in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Catalan, Basque and Galician.

Open source (GPLv3): github.com/Eduzyrtin/vESTER
